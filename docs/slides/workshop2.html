DRAFT OR REFINE?

When Is an AI Draft OK?

AI Drafts From Your Outline

When: routine, low stakes, and you know the content.

Meeting recaps, reminders, announcements, status updates, notes into a table.

You Write, AI Refines

When: your expertise, judgment, or voice is the point, or the stakes are high.

Grant aims, manuscript sections, recommendation letters, performance feedback, policy.

Leave AI Out

When: sensitive information, or the decision itself.

Student records or health information, who gets hired or funded, sources you haven't opened.

AI AS YOUR EDITOR

Three Ways to Refine What You Wrote

1 · Refine the languageMake this clearer and shorter without changing the meaning. List what you changed.
2 · Catch inconsistenciesCheck this for anything that doesn't match: dates, names, numbers, or a term used two ways. List them; don't rewrite.
3 · Find what's missingThis is a [type of document] for [audience]. What would they expect that isn't here? List it; don't add it.
Ask for a list, not a rewrite. The writing stays yours, and the suggestions are easy to check.

JOB A · A DOCUMENT

Summarize, Rewrite, Find the Gaps

Demo file · Workshop2_Demo_StudyDescription.docx

Pedestrian injuries remain a leading cause of traffic death in U.S. cities, and risk is concentrated at a small share of intersections. This study will link citywide crash records with street-level imagery to identify built-environment features — crossing distance, vehicle speeds, lighting, signal timing — associated with severe pedestrian injury at signalized intersections. Results will produce a ranked list of modifiable intersection features for city transportation partners…

Step 1 · SummarizeSummarize this document in 5 bullet points for a project status update.
Step 2 · Rewrite for an audienceRewrite the first paragraph for a community advisory board. No jargon, 8th-grade reading level, 4 sentences.
Step 3 · Find what's missingWhat would a funder expect in a study description that isn't here? List the gaps; don't rewrite.

Check it: did the plain version keep the facts right? Are the gaps it lists real ones?

ACTIVITY 2 · ~11 MIN

Your Own Task: Plan, Then Draft or Refine

  1. 1Plan first: 3–5 bullets in your words. The point, who it's for, the key facts.
  2. 2Decide: routine? AI drafts from your outline. Your expertise? You write, AI refines.
  3. 3Improve it with one or two follow-ups.
  4. 4Final edit: check it against your plan. Is everything there?
  5. 5Save the prompt in a “My prompts” document, with [blanks].
Recipe · draft from my outlineYou are a [role]. Write a [type of document] for [audience] from my outline below. Use only what's in the outline; mark anything you had to guess. [your bullets]
Recipe · refine my writingHere is my draft of a [type of document] for [audience]. List anything unclear, inconsistent, or missing. Don't rewrite it. [your draft]

WRAP-UP

What to Take Away

Plan first. Then AI drafts from your outline, or refines what you wrote. Then you check it.

  • Routine and low stakes: AI drafts from your outline. Your expertise or high stakes: you write, AI refines.
  • As an editor, AI tightens language, catches inconsistencies, and finds what's missing. Ask for a list, not a rewrite.
  • Before you use it: Verified? Disclosed? Yours?
  • Before Core 3: save two recipes (one draft, one refine) and use one again next week.
